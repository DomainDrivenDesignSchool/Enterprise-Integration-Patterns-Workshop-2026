# 🚀 Pre-Workshop Assignments

Welcome to the **Enterprise Integration Patterns Workshop**.

Before Session 1, every participant joins one of three groups.

Each group is responsible for building **one layer** of a shared architectural model.

Together, all groups will construct the workshop trilogy:

```
Message
    ↓
Flow
    ↓
Architecture
```

No single group can complete the picture alone.

Each group produces **one artifact**.

In Session 1, we will combine all artifacts into a single shared integration model and reveal the hidden structure of the system.

---

## 🧩 Group Responsibilities

### 🔵 Group 1 - The Messengers

You will build the **Enterprise Message Catalog**.

You answer one question:

> What is actually being communicated in the system?

---

### 🟠 Group 2 - The Flow Architects

You will build the **Enterprise Message Flow**.

You answer one question:

> How do messages move through the business over time?

---

### 🟢 Group 3 - The City Planners

You will build the **Enterprise Integration Landscape**.

You answer one question:

> Where does this movement actually happen, and what enables or blocks it?

---

## 📌 General Rules

- Work on **one real business process**
- Use **real systems and real situations**
- Observe before proposing solutions
- Do **not** redesign the architecture
- Do **not** read the Enterprise Integration Patterns book before Session 1
- Bring disagreements, confusion, and contradictions to the workshop

---

## 🎯 What This Is Really About

This is not a modeling exercise.

It is a **shared discovery process**.

The goal is not correctness.

The goal is to build a **shared mental model of integration**.

In Session 1, we will combine your three perspectives and see the system as a whole for the first time.
